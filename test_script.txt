The Last Lantern

A short mythological drama about a young temple keeper who must relight the sacred lantern before the gods abandon the village forever.

CHARACTERS:
- Kael: A 22-year-old temple keeper, lean and determined, with deep brown skin, short curly black hair, and kind dark eyes. He wears a simple white cotton tunic with a gold sash and carries a brass oil lantern.
- Mira: A 70-year-old village elder, wise and fragile, with silver hair in a long braid, weathered olive skin, and sharp green eyes. She wears a deep indigo robe with embroidered stars and leans on a gnarled wooden staff.
- The Guardian Spirit: A towering translucent figure made of blue-white light, with no face but a crown of floating golden shards. It speaks in echoes.

SCENE 1 — THE DYING LIGHT

NARRATOR: In the mountain village of Aethon, a sacred lantern has burned for a thousand years. Tonight, it flickers its last.

[Kael rushes through a stone temple corridor, lantern in hand, shadows growing around him.]

KAEL: The flame is dying. If it goes out, the gods will leave us forever.

[Mira stands at the temple entrance, silhouetted against a stormy sky.]

MIRA: You must climb to the summit. Only the Guardian Spirit can reignite the flame. But it will ask a price.

SCENE 2 — THE CLIMB

NARRATOR: Kael ascends the mountain through wind and rain, the lantern's light shrinking with every step.

[Kael climbs a narrow stone path carved into a cliff face, rain lashing against him, the tiny lantern glow barely visible.]

KAEL (breathing hard): I won't let it die. I promised my father.

SCENE 3 — THE SUMMIT

NARRATOR: At the peak, the Guardian Spirit materializes — vast, ancient, and silent.

[Kael stands before the towering Guardian Spirit on a flat mountain summit, storm clouds swirling around them, the spirit's golden shards casting scattered light.]

GUARDIAN SPIRIT: You carry the last light of Aethon. To restore it, you must give your memory of home. You will never remember why you climbed.

[Kael looks down at the lantern, then back at the spirit.]

KAEL: Then I'll find a new reason.

[The Guardian extends a hand of pure light. Kael holds out the lantern. A blinding flash fills the frame.]

SCENE 4 — THE RETURN

NARRATOR: The lantern burns bright again. Kael walks back down the mountain, smiling — though he cannot remember why.

[Kael descends the mountain path at dawn, golden sunlight breaking through clouds, the lantern glowing warm and strong. He looks at peace but slightly confused, as if waking from a dream.]

[Mira waits at the temple gate, tears in her eyes.]

MIRA: You did it. The gods will stay.

[Kael smiles gently, but there is no recognition in his eyes.]

KAEL: I'm sorry — have we met?

[Mira's expression shifts from joy to heartbreak. She places a hand on his cheek.]

MIRA: Every day, my boy. Every day.

[The sacred lantern blazes behind them, its light spilling across the village like a sunrise.]
