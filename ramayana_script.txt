The Mountain of Life

A retelling of the Ramayana — when Lakshmana falls to Ravana's weapon, Hanuman races against dawn to fetch the healing mountain from the Himalayas.

CHARACTERS:
- Hanuman: A towering Vanara warrior with dense reddish-orange fur and glowing red skin. Broad, hyper-muscular chest, a strong monkey-like face with a prominent jaw, burning golden eyes radiating pure devotion. He wears an ornate golden crown, heavy gold hoop earrings, thick gold necklaces over his bare chest, a bright red silk dhoti with a golden belt, and a long prehensile red tail. He carries a massive golden mace on his shoulder.
- Lakshmana: A tall, athletic prince with fair golden-toned skin, a sharp angular jawline, intense amber-brown eyes, dark brown hair tied in a tight topknot. He wears a simple saffron-orange ascetic dhoti, wooden bead necklace, leather armbands, and a quiver of arrows across his back. He lies wounded and unconscious.
- Rama: A noble prince with subtle blue-tinted skin, a calm divine oval face, deep compassionate dark brown eyes, long dark hair half-tied in a topknot. He wears a saffron-orange ascetic dhoti with a simple wooden bead necklace and rudraksha bracelets. He carries a massive carved dark wood bow.
- Sushena: An ancient Vanara physician with white fur, a wise weathered face, gentle dark eyes, and a long white beard. He wears simple earth-toned robes and carries a leather satchel of herbs.
- Ravana: A towering demon king with dark crimson-red skin, ten heads with ornate golden crowns, fierce glowing ember-orange eyes, heavy golden chest armor over crimson silk robes. He watches from afar with a cruel smile.

SCENE 1 — THE FALLEN WARRIOR

NARRATOR: On the battlefield of Lanka, struck by Indrajit's serpent arrow, Lakshmana collapses. His breath fades. The war halts.

[Rama kneels beside Lakshmana's unconscious body on a dusty battlefield at dusk, cradling his brother's head, anguish on his face. Fallen weapons and scattered arrows surround them.]

RAMA: Brother... open your eyes. I cannot lose you too.

[Sushena rushes in, examines Lakshmana, and shakes his head gravely.]

SUSHENA: Only the Sanjeevani herb from Mount Dronagiri can save him. But the mountain is in the Himalayas — a thousand leagues away. He has until dawn.

SCENE 2 — THE PROMISE

NARRATOR: Hanuman steps forward, the earth trembling beneath his feet.

[Hanuman stands before Rama and Sushena on the battlefield, golden mace gleaming in the firelight, his expression fierce and unwavering.]

HANUMAN: I will bring the mountain before the sun rises. Give me your blessing, my lord.

[Rama places his hand on Hanuman's shoulder, tears in his eyes.]

RAMA: Go. Fly faster than fate itself.

[Hanuman leaps skyward, a streak of orange and gold vanishing into the stars.]

SCENE 3 — THE FLIGHT

NARRATOR: Hanuman soars across the ocean, the wind roaring past him, Lanka shrinking behind.

[Hanuman flies through a vast night sky, clouds parting around his body, the moonlit ocean far below, his red dhoti and golden crown blazing against the darkness.]

[He passes over forests, rivers, and frozen peaks, growing closer to the distant Himalayas, snow-capped and silver under starlight.]

SCENE 4 — THE MOUNTAIN

NARRATOR: At Dronagiri, Hanuman cannot identify the herb. So he lifts the entire mountain.

[Hanuman stands before a massive snow-covered mountain peak, his hands gripping the base, muscles straining. With a mighty roar, he tears the mountain free from the earth, hoisting it above his head.]

[Hanuman flies back through the night sky, carrying the enormous mountain above his head like a crown, snow and rocks crumbling from its edges, his face lit with fierce determination.]

SCENE 5 — THE RETURN

NARRATOR: Hanuman returns before dawn. The herb is ground and placed on Lakshmana's lips. Color returns to his face. He breathes.

[Hanuman lands on the battlefield at first light, setting the mountain down with a thunderous crash. Sushena rushes to collect herbs from the mountain's base.]

[Sushena grinds the glowing green herb and applies it to Lakshmana's lips. Rama watches, hands clasped in prayer.]

[Lakshmana's eyes flutter open. He looks up at Rama and smiles weakly.]

LAKSHMANA: Brother... I dreamed I was far away.

[Rama pulls Lakshmana into a tight embrace, tears streaming down his face. Hanuman kneels behind them, head bowed, golden mace planted in the earth, a humble smile on his lips.]

NARRATOR: In the darkest hour, devotion moved a mountain. And dawn came not with war, but with reunion.

[Wide shot: the battlefield at sunrise, Rama embracing Lakshmana, Hanuman kneeling in prayer behind them, the Sanjeevani mountain glowing faintly green in the golden morning light.]
