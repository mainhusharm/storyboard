FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do the free credits work?
Every new account gets 60 credits — enough for roughly one 30-second film end-to-end (frames, video and narration). Local development is unlimited; credits only apply on the hosted app.
Will my character look the same in every shot?
Yes. With character consistency enabled, each frame is generated from your locked character reference portrait, so faces, hair, wardrobe and skin tone stay identical across the entire film.
Which video models can I use?
Choose per project between Grok Video (6s clips, fast), Omni Flash (8s clips) and Veo 3.1 (8s, highest quality). If a model rejects a shot, the suite repairs the prompt and retries automatically.
Can I narrate in a language other than English?
Yes — pick your language and a male or female narrator. Narration is generated per frame and mixed into the final film with adjustable volumes for voice and background audio.
Do I need any editing software?
No. The studio renders your shots, chains the scenes, adds narration and combines everything into a single finished video file you can publish straight away.
