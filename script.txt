RAMAYANA — THE LIGHT RETURNS

NARRATION: In the kingdom of Ayodhya, Prince Rama was exiled to the forest for fourteen years. His wife Sita and brother Lakshmana followed him into the wild.

Rama — a tall noble prince with blue-tinted skin, dark flowing hair, a calm divine face, wearing a simple saffron ascetic robe and carrying a great bow — walks into the Dandaka forest at golden hour, Sita and Lakshmana beside him.

Sita — a beautiful queen with long dark hair adorned with jasmine flowers, wearing a simple red and gold saree, large kohl-lined eyes — gathers wildflowers in a sunlit forest clearing.

NARRATION: From across the ocean, the demon king Ravana burned with desire for Sita.

Ravana — a towering fearsome figure with ten crowned heads, dark crimson skin, golden armor, a cruel smile, fierce glowing eyes — sits on his obsidian throne in Lanka, framed by fire and shadows.

Ravana: "I will have what the gods themselves could not protect."

NARRATION: Disguised as a golden deer, Ravana lured Rama away. When Sita stepped across the line of protection, he seized her.

A magnificent golden deer darts through the forest. Sita crosses a line drawn in the dirt. Dark storm clouds gather instantly. Ravana rises from the shadows in his true ten-headed form and lifts Sita into his flying chariot as she cries out to the sky.

Sita: "RAMA! The trees are my witness!"

NARRATION: Deep in the forest, the devoted warrior Hanuman met Rama and vowed to find Sita.

Hanuman — a powerful monkey-faced vanara warrior with a red body and glowing face, tremendous muscular build, a golden mace resting on his shoulder, loyal burning eyes — kneels before Rama in a shaft of divine light in the dark forest.

Hanuman: "I will cross the ocean itself, my lord."

NARRATION: With one mighty leap, Hanuman soared over the sea to the island of Lanka.

Hanuman leaps from a mountain peak, his red body blazing against a storm-dark sky, the vast ocean far below him, the golden city of Lanka glittering on the horizon at twilight.

NARRATION: In Ravana's palace gardens, Hanuman found Sita captive beneath a white ashoka tree.

Sita sits grief-stricken beneath an ashoka tree in a moonlit garden, surrounded by demoness guards. Hanuman hides in the branches above, offering her Rama's ring. Moonlight pours through silver leaves.

NARRATION: Rama and the monkey army built a bridge of stone across the ocean.

Thousands of vanara warriors hurl massive boulders into a raging sea, the stones floating and locking together to form a great stone bridge stretching to the horizon. Storm clouds break, revealing divine light. Rama stands at the bridge's head, bow raised.

NARRATION: War erupted. In the final battle, Rama faced the ten-headed king.

Rama stands on the battlefield at dawn, drawing his bow. A divine arrow of blazing light streaks across the sky toward Ravana. Fire, smoke, and dust fill the air. The ten-headed king raises ten swords but the light pierces his heart.

NARRATION: Lanka fell. Sita was free. And the exiled prince began the journey home.

Sita and Rama embrace in golden light on the battlefield as the smoke clears. Hanuman kneels beside them, head bowed.

NARRATION: Ayodhya lit a million lamps to welcome their king home. The exile was over. The light had returned.

The city of Ayodhya glows with countless oil lamps lining every rooftop and street. Rama and Sita, now in royal attire, walk together through the joyous crowds at night, every home ablaze with warm golden light. Stars fill the sky above.

Diwali is born.
